# Growth Opportunities

Twenty capabilities that could extend what Utility Connect already does
publicly. Each is a **hypothesis with a way to test it**, never a claim about
current performance or a promise of revenue.

Three rules hold throughout:

1. **No invented numbers.** No conversion lifts, no revenue projections, no
   customer counts. Where a number would be needed, the metric that would
   produce it is named instead.
2. **Additive, never a replacement.** Utility Connect has years of proprietary
   CRM, partner, white-label and workflow platform work behind it. Every idea
   below assumes that exists and attaches to it.
3. **Consent is a precondition, not a footnote.** Their public consent language
   is scoped to four named purposes and is revocable. Anything that contacts a
   customer inherits that constraint.

Every item carries a label: **BUILT AND FUNCTIONING** · **INTERACTIVE CONCEPT** ·
**FUTURE HYPOTHESIS**.

## What the market already validates

Studying adjacent public products establishes that the *demand* is real, which
is what makes these hypotheses worth testing rather than speculation:

- **LiveEasy** (formerly MoveEasy) markets a lifetime home-management and
  concierge platform, white-labelled for real-estate partners, with concierge
  service described as complimentary for life. `[FACT — verified]`
- **Updater** runs a moving marketplace and lets partners embed its commerce
  flows. `[FACT]`
- **Porch** combines checklists, concierge, memberships, appliance and warranty
  information, and home-service offers. `[FACT]`
- **Move Concierge** advertises partner revenue-sharing and bill review outside
  an active move. `[FACT]`

The strategic reading is **not** "copy these." It is:

> The market has validated that the relationship extends past move-in. The
> differentiation available to Utility Connect is not another home dashboard —
> it is being the one whose every handoff, offer, and attribution is
> *verifiable*.

That moat is the part already built.

## A · Revenue per move

### 1 · Move Wallet — `INTERACTIVE CONCEPT`
**Stakeholder** Customer · Provider · Partner
**Friction** Move-in benefits are scattered, unverifiable, and easy to mistake
for marketing. A customer cannot tell a funded credit from an advertisement.
**Capability** One transparent place for every eligible benefit, each showing
its funder, eligibility rule, terms, and expiry. An Offer Graph stores
provider, campaign, category, geography, eligibility, dates, budget, partner
restrictions, disclosures, and redemption state.
**Mechanism** Provider-funded promotions, partner-funded closing gifts,
builder move-in incentives.
**Metric to validate** Offers presented per move · acceptance rate ·
completion by category · attribution completeness.
**Data required** Real campaign terms and budgets from providers.
**Risk** Reads as an ad unit if funding is not disclosed. Mitigation: funder is
always on the face of the offer.
**Consent** Redemption is customer-initiated; no silent enrolment.
**Guardrail** AI may explain an offer in plain language. AI may never decide
hidden eligibility, invent a discount, or rank providers by payment.

### 2 · Embedded Commerce SDK — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Stakeholder** Enterprise partners
**Friction** Partners must send customers away to a separate flow, losing
context and completion.
**Capability** An embeddable service-selection flow partners host inside their
own portal or app, with attribution preserved through the embed.
**Metric** Referrals via embed vs redirect · completion rate by surface ·
integration errors.
**Risk** An embed multiplies the surface where PII can leak; it inherits the
same partner-safe projection rules as everything else.

### 3 · Service Bundle Composer — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Friction** Customers assemble services one call at a time, and dependencies
between them are invisible.
**Capability** Customer-selected combinations that surface real dependencies —
internet install before a security panel, electricity before appliance
delivery — while preserving provider choice and disclosures.
**Metric** Services per move · dependency conflicts caught before scheduling.
**Guardrail** Bundles must never obscure that each provider is contracted
separately, which their Terms make explicit.

### 4 · Renewal and Bill Review — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Friction** Introductory pricing lapses silently, months after anyone was
paying attention.
**Capability** A permissioned check-in when a promotional period is ending or
when a customer asks for a review.
**Metric** Review requests · renewal interactions · repeat service connections.
**Consent** Requires an explicit ongoing-contact purpose beyond the four in the
current intake wording — this cannot ride on move-in consent.

### 5 · Sponsored Move-In Programs — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Builders and brokerages fund transparent new-home benefits for
their communities.
**Metric** Program adoption · benefit redemption · partner renewal.
**Risk** Sponsorship must never alter provider ranking. Funding is disclosed;
ordering is not for sale.

## B · Reach per partner

### 6 · Network Launchpad — `INTERACTIVE CONCEPT`
**Stakeholder** Partnerships · Engineering
**Friction** Every new partner arrives with a different payload shape, so
onboarding effort scales linearly with partner count.
**Capability** Sample payload → AI-assisted field mapping → deterministic
validation → generated contract tests → synthetic referrals → white-label
preview → **human approval** → launch → drift monitoring.
**Metric** Time to first successful synthetic referral · mappings accepted
without engineering · drift incidents caught pre-production.
**Guardrail** AI suggests mappings and explains errors. AI never activates an
integration, sets authorization, or validates consent.
**Already built** The contract layer and quarantine underneath it.

### 7 · Brokerage Campaign Studio — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** A brokerage composes a branded program — first-time buyer,
renter, relocation — with its own services, benefits, and follow-up.
**Metric** Campaigns launched without engineering · referrals per campaign.

### 8 · Builder Community Launch Kit — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Configure an entire development once: providers, services,
neighbourhood information, handover experience.
**Metric** Time to launch a community · homes connected per community.
**Why it fits** Their public site names Builders & HOAs as a served industry
and states they integrate with city municipalities.

### 9 · Partner Health Scorecard — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Measured data quality, referral completeness, integration error
rate, and follow-through — surfaced to the partner.
**Risk** This is where a fabricated "AI score" would be tempting and wrong.
Every figure must trace to counted events or it does not ship.

### 10 · Attribution and Commission Evidence — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Friction** Attribution disputes are resolved by whoever has the better
spreadsheet.
**Capability** A traceable explanation of referral source, channel, timestamp,
and service completion — without exposing internal customer data.
**Metric** Disputes raised · disputes resolved from the record alone.
**Already built** Field-level provenance and the partner-safe projection are
the foundation this needs.

## C · Customer lifetime

### 11 · Home Continuum — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** A permissioned relationship after move-in: installation
verification at 7 days, plan review at 6 months, renewal at 11–12 months, and
life events (sale, remodel, new tenant, next move).
**Metric** 6- and 12-month engagement · repeat use at the next move.
**Consent** Every trigger checks purpose, channel, and frequency at send time
against the live ledger. No silent marketing automation.

### 12 · Home Passport — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** A customer-controlled record of connected services, account
references, installation dates, warranty documents, and approved vendors.
**Why it differentiates** Portability. The customer owns it and can take it to
the next move — which makes the next move Utility Connect's to win.

### 13 · Home Readiness Score — `INTERACTIVE CONCEPT`
**Capability** A deterministic checklist: what is confirmed, what needs the
customer, what threatens move-in readiness.
**Guardrail** Deterministic. A "readiness score" produced by a model would be
unexplainable at exactly the moment it matters.
**Metric** Readiness at T-7 days · customer actions completed after prompting.

### 14 · Customer Referral Loop — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** A satisfied mover refers another, with any benefit explicitly
disclosed.
**Risk** Referral incentives are regulated in real-estate-adjacent contexts;
this needs legal review before it is more than a hypothesis.

### 15 · Seasonal Home Care — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Opt-in maintenance moments and trusted-vendor introductions
rather than untargeted campaigns.
**Metric** Opt-in rate · vendor jobs completed · repeat engagement.

## D · Operational leverage

### 16 · Concierge Compiler — `INTERACTIVE CONCEPT`
**Stakeholder** Concierge · Operations
**Friction** Context is retyped after every call, and detail is lost.
**Capability** A call becomes evidence-linked facts, each tied to a transcript
utterance, speaker, and timestamp. The concierge sees confirmed facts,
conditional interests, contradictions, and the next best question.
**Guardrail** The AI never updates the canonical record. The concierge
confirms, and the merge runs through the same human-approval path already
enforced by a database constraint.
**Metric** After-call work · repeat contacts · facts captured per call.

### 17 · Next-Best-Question Engine — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Suggest the highest-value clarification given open conflicts,
move urgency, and service dependencies.
**Metric** Open unknowns at call end · second-call rate.

### 18 · Quality and Coaching Lab — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Review appropriately governed calls for missed disclosures and
training opportunities.
**Consent** Recording and review carry their own consent and retention rules —
this is not covered by service-setup consent.

### 19 · AI Operations Brain — `FUTURE HYPOTHESIS`
**Capability** Leadership asks operational questions and receives answers that
**cite operational records**.
> Not: "The AI believes customers prefer Provider A."
> Instead: "18 records were delayed by missing apartment-unit information; here
> are the workflow events."
**Metric** Questions answered from cited data vs escalated to analysts.

### 20 · Scenario Compiler — `INTERACTIVE CONCEPT`
**Capability** A leader describes a failure in plain language — "500 referrals,
10% missing consent timestamps, one provider times out after creating the
order" — and the system generates synthetic referrals, injects the failures,
runs permission tests, and produces a pass/fail replay. An engineer approves
the generated scenario before it executes.
**Why it is unusual** It is simultaneously product validation, incident
reproduction, integration certification, AI regression testing, and a sales
demonstration.
**Already built** The `scenario.test.ts` suite and the Failure Theater are a
working seed of exactly this.

## How to read this list

Five of the twenty (**1, 6, 13, 16, 20**) already have their hardest
substrate built: provenance, contracts and quarantine, human-gated merge, the
consent ledger, and the failure-injection harness. They are concepts because
the *surface* is unbuilt, not because the foundation is missing.

The rest are honest hypotheses. Each names the metric that would prove or kill
it, the data required, and the risk that would make it a bad idea. That is the
difference between a roadmap and a wish list — and it is the same discipline
the rest of this project applies to its own claims.
