## What is deliberately not here

No photograph, illustration, icon or other asset belonging to Utility Connect.
Their images are theirs; this project is not affiliated with them, has no
licence to republish their work, and says so in the README and the LICENSE.

The hero resembles their art direction because it is *graded* to match it —
desaturated under a navy wash, darkened from the left so white type holds its
contrast — not because it was taken from them. That grade lives in
`src/components/HomeScene.tsx` and is applied to an image we are entitled to use.

Renders under `public/renders/` are ours, produced from the 3D residence in
`/story` by `window.__captureHero()` in development.
